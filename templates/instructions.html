Emotion Labeling Task

Thank you for participating! In this task you will read short text messages and label the emotion each one expresses.

  • You will label 5 messages, shown one at a time.
  • For each message, choose the single emotion that best describes what the writer is feeling: anger, fear, joy, love, sadness, or surprise.
  • There are no right or wrong answers. Just go with your first impression!
  • The task takes about 2 minutes. Your responses are recorded anonymously with a random participant ID.
